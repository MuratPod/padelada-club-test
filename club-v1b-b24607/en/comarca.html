What the first one gets

  • Play the first verified game in the comarca and keep the score yourself. Once the comarca has 16 club members, the “Founder” title is yours.
  • A permanent “Founder” badge on your profile: a teal ring and the caption “Comarca founder · Ripollès”.
  • Your name on the comarca page for good: “Comarca founder: …”.
  • Padelada Plus free for life.
  • Comarca captain automatically once it has 16 club members, until the end of the first season. After that, the captain is chosen by the general rules.

The founder is whoever keeps the score of the comarca’s first verified game: counts it on the watch or phone and asks the others to confirm. The title becomes real once the comarca has 16 club members — enough for a tournament. The other players in that first game get the “Since day one” mark.
