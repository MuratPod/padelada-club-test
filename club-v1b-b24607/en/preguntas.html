Questions

Can I buy a place in the club?

No. A place in the club is given only by the game.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The club is free, and a place in it can’t be bought.

What happens if I haven't played for a long time?

You stay a club member. Membership is for life.

What can you lose membership for?

Only for proven cheating: fake games, a fake photo or a fake name. A break from playing doesn’t cost you it.

How do you become a captain?

The comarca’s first captain is whoever kept the score of its first verified game, once the comarca has 16 club members. They are also its founder for good and stay captain until the end of the first season. After that, nobody appoints captains: the club notices the most active player and offers them the captaincy. A captain stays captain for as long as they play; as soon as they stop, the role passes to the next player by itself. It can’t be requested or bought.

Is the comarca founder the captain?

The comarca’s first captain is its founder. But they are two separate titles: captain is a post that changes hands, founder is for good: a teal ring, their name on the comarca page and Plus free for life. The founder stays captain automatically until the end of the first season; after that, the captain is chosen by the general rules. Neither can be bought.

What does the captain get?

A gold card and the respect of the comarca. Being captain is unpaid.

What happens if the captain stops playing?

After a month without games the app warns them; after two it pauses them, and the role is offered to the next player.

Can a captain be reported?

Yes. Every complaint is checked by a referee, and only the ones they confirm count. After three complaints confirmed by a referee, the Padelada team decides whether they stay captain. If not, the club offers the role to the next player.

Who keeps order in the comarca chat?

Mostly the chat itself. Club members write in it, and someone who never starts playing only reads after 10 days or 20 messages. A club member has a coloured card, a guest has a plain one, and you can see it at once. The comarca captain handles pinned messages and spam, and any message can be reported.

Who can call the Referee?

The Referee is for club members.

Who sees my name and photo on this site?

Only other club members, after signing in. Everyone else sees only the total number of club members in the comarca.

Why am I not in the comarca ranking?

The ranking lists every club member in this comarca. If you’re not there, check the comarca in your profile in the app.

When are the seasons?

Two seasons a year: April to September and October to March.

How do I sign up for a tournament?

Only in the app. There’s no paid entry to tournaments.

Can I buy anything on this site?

No. Nothing is sold on padelada.club.

How to get in

Two games, four people, one week.
