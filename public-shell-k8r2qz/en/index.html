FAQ

What is Club Padelada?

A padel community organised by comarca, with matches and tournaments in the app.

How do I join?

Download the app, pick your comarca and you're in.

What does the captain do?

Runs comarca life: matches, tournaments and new members.

How is the Founder chosen?

Whoever invites player 16. In a tie, server time decides.

Can I play in another comarca?

Yes, in open tournaments; your comarca is your home area.
