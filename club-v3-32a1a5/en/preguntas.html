Questions

How do I get into the club?

Play two verified games within seven days with four different people, and put your real name and photo in your profile. There are no forms and no approvals: the game itself lets you in.

Can I buy a place in the club?

No. A place can’t be bought or won through connections; only the game earns it.

How do I become captain of a comarca?

Tap “Become a captain” and you’ll land in the app. You can become captain in the comarca where you are right now and have already played. Accept the terms and the comarca chat opens.

What happens if the captain stops playing?

After a month without games the app warns the captain; after two it pauses the captaincy and offers the role to the next person.

Who is the Founder of a comarca?

The title “Founder of the comarca” is decided by a contest between candidates. Only players who arrived through a candidate’s link and joined the club count. Whoever is first to gather 16 wins. Captain and Founder of the comarca are different titles.
